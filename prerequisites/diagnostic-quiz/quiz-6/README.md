### Question 2.
```math
\begin{aligned}
\log_2{16}&=8 \times 2^{-x} \\
\log_2{2^4}&=2^3 \times 2^{-x} \\
4 &= 2^3 \times 2^{-x} \\
2^2 &= 2^{3-x} \\
2 &= 3-x \\
x &= 1 \\
\therefore \ E
\end{aligned}
```
