# Diagnostic Test 1: Algebraic Skills I

## Questions

### Question 1.
Solve $\frac{a^3+b^3}{c^2+d^2}$ where $a=2$, $b=-2$, $c=4$, $d=-2$. <br>
(A) $\frac{4}{5}$ <br>
(B) $\frac{4}{3}$ <br>
(C) $\frac{3}{4}$ <br>
(D) $\frac{5}{4}$ <br>
(E) $0$ <br>
(F) None of the above

## Answers

### Question 1.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    \frac{a^3+b^3}{c^2+d^2}
    &= \frac{2^3+(-2)^3}{4^2+(-2)^2} \\
    &= \frac{8+(-8)}{16+4} \\
    &= \frac{0}{20} \\
    &= 0 \\
    \therefore\ E
\end{aligned}
```
