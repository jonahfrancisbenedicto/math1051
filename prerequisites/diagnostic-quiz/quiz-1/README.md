## Answers

### Question 1.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    \frac{a^3+b^3}{c^2+d^2}
    &= \frac{2^3+(-2)^3}{4^2+(-2)^2} \\
    &= \frac{8+(-8)}{16+4} \\
    &= \frac{0}{20} \\
    &= 0 \\
    \therefore\ E
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 2.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    \frac{x^8x^{-2}}{x^{-6}x^2} &= \frac{x^6}{x^{-4}} \\
    &= x^{10} \\
    \therefore\ F
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 3.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    x(x+y)(x-y) &= x(x^2-y^2) \\
    &= x^3-xy^2 \\
    \therefore\ E
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 4.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    C &= \frac{5}{9}(F-32) \\
    \frac{9}{5}C &= F-32 \\
    \frac{9}{5}C+32 &= F \\
    F &= \frac{9}{5}C+32 \\
    \therefore\ C
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 5.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    \frac{a}{2} \div \frac{b}{2} \times \frac{c}{2} &= \frac{a}{2} \times \frac{2}{b} \times \frac{c}{2} \\
    &= \frac{2ac}{4b} \\
    &= \frac{ac}{2b} \\
    \therefore\ B
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 6. 
```math
\begin{aligned}
    \frac{2}{x} \div \frac{2}{y} &= \frac{y}{x} \\
    \frac{2}{x} \times \frac{y}{2} &= \frac{y}{x} \\
    \frac{2y}{2x} &= \frac{y}{x} \\
    \frac{y}{x} &= \frac{y}{x} \\
    \therefore\ E
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 7.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    \frac{4x+12}{x+3} &= \frac{4(x+3)}{x+3} \\
    &= 4 \\
    \therefore\ A
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 8.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    2x-5 &= 5x+4 \\
    -9 &= 3x \\
    -3 &= x \\
    x &= -3 \\
    \therefore\ -3
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 9.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    \frac{x+1}{-2x+1} &= \frac{3}{4} \\
    4(x+1) &= 3(-2x+1) \\
    4x + 4 &= -6x+3 \\
    10x = -1 \\
    x = -0.1 \\
    \therefore\ -0.1
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 10.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    3+\frac{x}{2} &= -5 \\
    \frac{x}{2} &= -8 \\
    x &= -16 \\
    \therefore\ -16
\end{aligned}
```
