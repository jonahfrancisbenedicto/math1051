# Diagnostic Test 1: Algebraic Skills I

## Questions

### Question 1.
Solve $\frac{a^3+b^3}{c^2+d^2}$ where $a=2$, $b=-2$, $c=4$, $d=-2$. <br>
(A) $\frac{4}{5}$ <br>
(B) $\frac{4}{3}$ <br>
(C) $\frac{3}{4}$ <br>
(D) $\frac{5}{4}$ <br>
(E) $0$ <br>
(F) None of the above

### Question 2.
Simplify $\frac{x^8x^{-2}}{x^{-6}x^2}$. <br>
(A) $x^2$ <br>
(B) $x^{14}$ <br>
(C) $x^{-2}$ <br>
(D) $x^{-14}$ <br>
(E) $x^{-10}$ <br>
(F) None of the above

### Question 3.
Expand and simplify $x(x+y)(x-y)$. <br>
(A) $x^3 - y^2$ <br>
(B) $x^3 + xy^2$ <br>
(C) $x^3 -xy^2 -2xy$ <br>
(D) $x^3 -xy^2 +2xy$ <br>
(E) $x^3 -xy^2$ <br>
(F) None of the above

## Answers

### Question 1.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    \frac{a^3+b^3}{c^2+d^2}
    &= \frac{2^3+(-2)^3}{4^2+(-2)^2} \\
    &= \frac{8+(-8)}{16+4} \\
    &= \frac{0}{20} \\
    &= 0 \\
    \therefore\ E
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 2.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    \frac{x^8x^{-2}}{x^{-6}x^2} &= \frac{x^6}{x^{-4}} \\
    &= x^{10} \\
    \therefore\ F
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 3.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    x(x+y)(x-y) &= x(x^2-y^2) \\
    &= x^3-xy^2 \\
    \therefore\ E
\end{aligned}
```
