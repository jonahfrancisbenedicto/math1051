### Question 8.
```math
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{3} \times (\frac{-3}{2})^3 \div 3^2 &= \frac{1}{2} + \frac{3^2}{2} \div 3^2 \\

### Question 9.

    \therefore \ y=4x+1
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 10.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    \text{(1)} 0=5x-y-4 \quad \text{(2)} x+2y=14 \\
    \text{(1)} y=5x-4 \\
    \text{Subtitute (1) into (2)} \\
    x+2(5x-4)=14 \\
    x+10x-8=14 \\
    11x=22 \\
    x=2 \\
    y=5x-4 \\
    y=5(2)-4 \\
    y=10-4 \\
    y=6 \\
    \therefore \ x=2 \text{and} y=6
\end{aligned}
```
