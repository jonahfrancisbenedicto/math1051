# Diagnostic Test 5: Indices

## Questions 

### Question 1.
Solve $(-2.7)^0$. <br>
(A) $0$ <br>
(B) $-1$ <br>
(C) $1$ <br>
(D) $-2.7$

### Question 2.
Solve $(4)^{-2}$. <br>
(A) $-\frac{1}{16}$ <br>
(B) $16$ <br>
(C) $\frac{1}{16}$ <br>
(D) $-8$

### Question 3.
Solve $(\frac{3x}{2})^4$. <br>
(A) $\frac{81x^4}{16}$ <br>
(B) $6x^4$ <br>
(C) $\frac{12x^4}{8}$ <br>
(D) $\frac{81x^4}{2}$

### Question 4.
Solve $y=15\times3^x$ where $x=-3$. <br>
(A) $\frac{5}{27}$ <br>
(B) $\frac{5}{3}$ <br>
(C) $\frac{5}{9}$ <br>
(D) $-135$

### Question 5.
Simplify $2^{\frac{5}{2}}-2^{\frac{3}{2}}$. <br>
(A) $2^{\frac{1}{2}}$ <br>
(B) $2$ <br>
(C) $2^{\frac{3}{2}}$ <br>
(D) $2^{\frac{5}{3}}$ <br>
(E) $2^2$

### Question 6.
Solve $f(x)=2^{-x}+1$ where $f(a)=3$.

### Question 7.
Solve $4\times(\frac{1}{3})^x=324$.

### Question 8.
Solve $4^{x+1}=(\frac{1}{8})^x$.

### Question 9.
Solve $(\frac{2}{3})^{-3}$.

### Question 10.
Solve $2^x\times8^{1-x}=\frac{1}{4}$.

## Answers

### Question 1.
```math
\begin{aligned}
(-2.7)^0&=1 \\
\therefore \ C
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 2.
```math
\begin{aligned}
(4)^{-2}&=\frac{1}{4^2} \\
&=\frac{1}{16} \\
\therefore \ C
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 3.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    (\frac{3x}{2})^4 &= \frac{81x^4}{16} \\
    \therefore \ A
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 4.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    y&=15 \times 3^x \\
    y&=15 \times 3^{-3} \\
    y&=15 \times \frac{1}{27} \\
    y&=\frac{15}{27} \\
    y&=\frac{5}{9} \\
    \therefore \ C
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 5. 
```math
\begin{aligned}
    2^{\frac{5}{2}}-2^{\frac{3}{2}} &= 2^{\frac{3}{2}}(2-1) \\
    &=2^{\frac{3}{2}} \\
    \therefore \ C
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 6.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    f(x)&=2^{-x}+1 \\
    3&=2^{-a}+1 \\
    2^{1}&=2^{-a} \\
    1&=-a \\
    a&=-1 \\
    \therefore \ a=-1
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 7.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    4 \times (\frac{1}{3})^x &= 324 \\
    (\frac{1}{3})^x &= 81 \\
    3^{-x}&=3^4 \\
    x&=-4 \\
    \therefore \ x=-4
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 8.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    4^{x+1}&=(\frac{1}{8})^x \\
    2^{2x+2}&=2^{-3x} \\
    2x+2&=-3x \\
    5x&=-2 \\
    x&=-\frac{2}{5}
    \therefore \ x=-\frac{2}{5}
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 9.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    (\frac{2}{3})^{-3} &= \frac{27}{8}
\end{aligned}
```

### Question 10.
```math
\begin{aligned}
    2^x \times 8^{1-x}&=\frac{1}{4} \\
    2^x \times 2^{3-3x}&=2^{-2} \\
    2^{3-2x}&=2^{-2} \\
    3-2x&=-2 \\
    5&=2x \\
    x&=\frac{5}{2} \\
    \therefore \ \frac{5}{2}
\end{aligned}
```
